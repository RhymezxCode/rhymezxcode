01 About

Native Android, taken all the way to production.

I’m a senior Android engineer with over four years of experience creating mobile applications with the Android framework in Java and Kotlin.

I’ve taken products from an idea to a Play Store release — ride-hailing, healthcare booking, vendor tools, and field data collection — and I publish libraries other developers can drop into their own apps.

I lead HackerCode Community, mentor junior Android developers, and contribute through the Google Dev Library.
