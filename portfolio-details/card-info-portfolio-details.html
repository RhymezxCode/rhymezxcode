Card Information Finder

Look up your own card’s brand, type, bank, and country from the BIN, by typing or scanning the number.

Category
Application

What I did

  • Built an app that looks up information about your own ATM card through the binlist API.
  • Shows the card brand, card type, bank name, and country.
  • The number can be typed or captured with an on-device OCR scan, then confirmed before the lookup.

Stack

  • Kotlin
  • binlist API
  • OCR
